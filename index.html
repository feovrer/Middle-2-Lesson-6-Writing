      Plan 표의 내용이 글 속 어디에 들어갔는지 색으로 확인해 보세요.

      The Greatest Love for All: Lee Tae-seok

      Do you know Lee Tae-seok? He was a doctor and a priest. He was born in Busan, Korea, in 1962. In 2001, he went to Tonj, a small village in South Sudan. There, he built a hospital and took care of sick people day and night. He also built a school and taught the children music. He made a brass band with them to give them hope. He was so kind that everyone in the village loved him. His life was so touching that I read his story again and again. From him, I learned that small acts of love can change people's lives. He also taught me that I should help people in need. I want to share my love with people around me like him.

      잘 쓰는 비법

      • ⭐ 핵심 문법 so ~ that: 물결 밑줄 문장을 찾아보세요. "너무 ~해서 …하다"
      • 첫 문장은 질문이나 직업 소개로 시작해요. Do you know ~?
      • 한 일을 이어 줄 때: also, There, Later
      • 마지막 문장은 나의 다짐: I want to ~ like him/her.
